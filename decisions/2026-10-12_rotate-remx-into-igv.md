## Rationale

Rotate REMX into IGV for the next 3–12 months. REMX's substantial lithium exposure leaves it vulnerable to weak commodity prices and excess supply, with limited evidence of a sustained recovery. IGV offers diversified software exposure with stronger absolute and relative momentum. As of 9 October, IGV was above its 20-, 50- and 200-day moving averages, while REMX was below all three; the IGV/REMX ratio also favoured the switch. The aim is to improve capital allocation rather than wait for REMX to recover. Main risks are a sharp commodity rebound and a reversal in software valuations or momentum.
